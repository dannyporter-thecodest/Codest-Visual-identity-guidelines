## What Cody does with it

- **Title** from 1.1 + 4.1 + 3.2: "Estimote turned its sensor-based technology into a space management application".
- **Statistics:** 6.1 has figures but no before figures or sources. Under `rules.md` section 3, a figure with neither is a question, not page content, so for a new case study Cody would ask Delivery first: "6.1 lists 60% faster occupancy tracking and EUR 120K saved annually with no before figure or source. What were they measured against, and who confirmed them?" (The published Estimote example shows them because it was converted from an existing page, where the conversion rules keep every published figure.)
- **Team line:** 5.3 is empty, so Approach has no team line and Cody asks: "5.3 is empty. What was the team (roles and number)?"
- **Quote:** 7.3 is approved, so it is used word for word.
