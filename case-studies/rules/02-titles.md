# 02. Titles

Choose by the strongest evidence the case has. Formulas 1 and 2 need a statistic from the source; 3 to 6 need none. The Codest patterns are shapes to follow, not claims.

| # | Use when | Formula | TSH example | Codest pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Before and after figures | [Client] cut [what] from [before] to [after] | xpate cut payout time for e-commerce merchants from 7 days to minutes | [Client] cut [process] time from [X] to [Y] |
| 2 | One statistic | [Client]'s [metric] [rose/fell] by [x%] | Travelia's user adoption rose by 31,6% and maintenance costs fell by 40% | [Client]'s [metric] fell by [x%] |
| 3 | A clear change, no number | [Client] turned [old thing] into [new thing] | Reservix turned its CMS into an AI-driven sales engine | Estimote turned its Bluetooth sensor technology into a workplace safety platform during the COVID-19 pandemic |
| 4 | Delivered against a deadline | [Client] [launched/scaled] [what] in [time] or on time | StageClip scaled up their SaaS product on time to onboard 500 new clients | [Client] launched [platform] in [time] |
| 5 | Old way replaced | [Client] replaced [old way] with [new way] | (closest is formula 3) | A logistics company replaced manual route planning with one platform |
| 6 | Unnamed client | [Descriptor] [verb] [what] | SEPA certification enabled one bank to reach 36+ countries | A Nordic payments company replaced its legacy ledger |

## Picking a formula

- The source has a before and after figure: formula 1.
- The source has one strong statistic: formula 2, or formula 3 and keep the statistic in Results.
- The story is a transformation: formula 3. This is the safest default.
- The story is speed: formula 4, only if the timeline is in the source.
- The client is not named: formula 6, with a factual descriptor (industry, region, size).

## Check every title

- Client first. The Codest is not named.
- 10 to 19 words.
- Past tense verb: cut, launched, turned, replaced, rebuilt, scaled, delivered.
- Any number in the title also appears, exactly as written, in Results.
- No hype: no "revolutionised", "transformed the industry", "game-changing".
- No casual hedges: no "in about", "around", "roughly".
