# 03. Writing register

Formal, precise and professional. Write as a senior consultancy reporting to a client's board, not as a blog or a sales page.

## Rules

- Full, complete sentences. No casual idioms, dramatic phrasing or rhetorical lines.
- Precise verbs: delivered, engineered, implemented, integrated, enabled, required, maintained.
- "The Codest" in the third person in the hook, impact statement and Project description. "We" is allowed in Approach.
- Facts stated plainly. If a sentence would need a fact the source does not have, leave the sentence out.
- British English (organisation, programme, optimise).
- No em dashes or en dashes. Use a comma, a colon or a new sentence.
- No exclamation marks.
- No vague intensifiers: significantly, greatly, hugely, truly, incredibly.
- No marketing filler: seamless, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, game-changer, leverage, empower, top-notch, world-class, robust (unless technical and specific).

## Too casual, and the professional version

These were flagged in review. Use the right-hand column as the standard.

| Too casual | Professional |
|---|---|
| A safety platform that arrived after the peak would have arrived too late to protect anyone. | Any delay in delivery would have reduced the platform's value to organisations managing workplace safety during the pandemic. |
| ...built and running while the need was urgent. | ...delivered within the timeframe of the pandemic response. |
| Sensitive data: contact and health information needed strong protection from day one. | Data security: the platform processed health-related data, requiring strong encryption, strict access controls and ongoing regulatory compliance. |
| We built both the frontend and the backend. | The Codest was responsible for both frontend and backend development. |
| The backend was built for scale: it takes in sensor data as it arrives... | The backend was engineered for scalability, supporting high volumes of real-time sensor data while maintaining consistent performance. |
| ...a workplace safety platform in about 3 months | ...a workplace safety platform during the COVID-19 pandemic (timeline goes in Results, from the source) |
| ...with The Codest owning the software | (The Codest is not named in the title) |

## Key challenge bullets

Pattern: `Label: one sentence stating the requirement and why it mattered.`

- Good: "Real-time monitoring: the system had to track interactions and distances between users in real time across large office spaces."
- Too casual: "Real time: it had to work live, all the time."
