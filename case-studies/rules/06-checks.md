# 07. Checks before handing back

`build.js` checks the items marked *(build)*. Cody checks the rest by reading the output.

## Content

- [ ] Every section is in the right order, with the right heading. *(build)*
- [ ] Word counts inside the ranges in `01-format.md`, or the shortfall explained in the report. *(build, warning)*
- [ ] Title names what was built or changed, follows a formula, client first, The Codest not named, past tense verb.
- [ ] Title is 8 to 14 words and 3 lines or fewer, reads in one breath, one outcome only. *(build)*
- [ ] Title does not start with a number or an outcome verb, and no statistic is its point. *(build)*
- [ ] Hook states the problem; impact statement states the value delivered. One sentence each. *(build)*
- [ ] About heading uses the client's name, or "About the client". *(build)*
- [ ] Stats figure has Industry, Headquarters and Tech stack. *(build)*
- [ ] Stats band numbers were read after the counters stopped (two matching reads).
- [ ] Every statistic on the source appears in Results or the stats band, exactly as written. *(build)*
- [ ] Any figure in the title, hook or impact statement is also in Results. *(build)*
- [ ] Results has 3 to 5 bullets; stats band has 0 or 2 to 4 figures. *(build)*
- [ ] Quote matches the source exactly. *(build)*
- [ ] Every fact can be found in `source.md` (converting) or `intake.md` (creating).
- [ ] Every name for the client, the system, its features, technologies and processes uses the source's own words. Nothing renamed or re-described.
- [ ] Reads as formal and professional: compare with `examples/estimote/case-study.md`.

## Hard rules

- [ ] No MUFG or Allegro, in any form. *(build, names only)*
- [ ] No em dashes or en dashes. *(build)*
- [ ] Plain characters only: no currency symbols, arrows, multiplication or copyright signs, or accented letters. *(build)*
- [ ] No banned filler words. *(build, warning)*
- [ ] Client named only if the source names them.

## Page

- [ ] Looked at at 1440px and 375px (`--shot`). Nothing overlaps, nothing is cut off.
- [ ] Compared with `examples/estimote/page.html`.

## Report
