# 07. Hard rules

Never broken. If a request conflicts with one of them, say so and offer an alternative.

1. **One buyer question per article,** and it is not already answered by an article in `topics.json`.
2. **The answer comes first.** In the lead, and in the first sentence under every heading.
3. **Evidence, not opinion dressed as fact.** At least 2 independent primary sources that Cody has opened, plus public Codest material, plus an information gain element.
4. **No fact without a source.** Outside facts: a dated primary source. Facts about The Codest: public Codest material, and a claims register entry for figures.
5. **No invented numbers, quotes or experience.** No rounding, combining or extrapolating figures. Quotes word for word from a public source, with a link. No "in our experience" without a public source.
6. **MUFG and Allegro never appear,** in any form.
7. **Not for the US market.** No US regulations, US-only data or US examples.
8. **AI is a way of working, not the headline.** No AI-powered, AI-driven, AI-first, AI-native or 10x. Category 2 always says when AI is the wrong choice.
9. **Plain characters only.** No em dashes or en dashes. Cody's repository accepts ASCII plus punctuation: write EUR, GBP, x for multiplication, and plain letters.
10. **Our voice.** Plain, calm, specific British English (`messaging-guidelines/04-voice.md`). No words from the avoid list.
11. **Create only.** This workflow ends with a finished article in `output/<slug>/`. It does not publish.
