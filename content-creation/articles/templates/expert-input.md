# Expert input

Send this to the Codest engineer or Delivery lead who will review the article. Written answers or a 10 to 15 minute call both work (Cody can clean up a call transcript). Short, honest answers are better than polished ones. This is the part of the article no competitor can copy, and the main reason AI assistants will cite it.

Save the answers as `content-creation/articles/output/<slug>/expert-input.md`.

## Questions

1. What is your short answer to the question, in one or two sentences?
2. When is the answer different? Give one situation where you would say the opposite.
3. What do you see go wrong most often? What is the first sign of it?
4. Tell me about one real situation from your work (no client names unless the case study is public). What happened, what did you do, what changed?
5. What would you tell a CTO or Head of Delivery to check or ask before deciding?
6. What do most articles on this topic get wrong or leave out?
7. Is there a number you trust here (a time, a cost, a ratio)? Where does it come from?

## Approval

Quotes used word for word in the article are sent back to you before publication. You can change or remove them.
