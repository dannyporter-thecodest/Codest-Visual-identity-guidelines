About this document

The identity as it exists today

A light, calm page in white and pale tints, with deep navy for headings and one loud accent, pink, for every action. Headings and buttons use a heavy, wide monospaced face that says "engineers". Body text uses a plain humanist sans. Corners are always square. The signature detail is a thin pink outline set 4px behind every button. Fine line-waves sit in the background as texture.

This is a record of what is there, not a new direction. Where something breaks accessibility or the 2026 brand strategy, it is flagged and left as it is.

Source

The current website design file in Figma: every page in desktop (1440px) and mobile (375px). Colour and type values come from the file's own variables. Component specs come from the file's button, tag and input layers. A few measurements were read from frame positions or screenshots and are marked as such.

What this does not cover

Words. Copy, claims and tone live in the content guide. Several headlines in the Figma mockups break that guide; see Things to fix.

Where the files are

Everything shown here is in the brand repository: tokens, fonts, logo, icons, wave textures and components under design-system/, and these guidelines as Markdown under visual-identity/.

Logo

The wordmark

Black on white and the pale tints (fill #231F20, as drawn). White on navy and dark photo heroes. Files: design-system/assets/logo/.

THE C0DEST, with a zero for the O, over the strapline IN CODE WE TRUST in small spaced capitals.

  • Use the files as they are. Never retype the wordmark, recolour it to pink or purple, add effects or separate the strapline.
  • On the site: 144 × 36px, at the left content edge of the header.
  • The file sets no minimum size or clear space. Recommended until decided: at least 96px wide, and the strapline's height clear on every side.
  • There is no navy logo and no standalone mark or favicon in the file. Do not create one.

Why pink matters most. Pink carries every button and link. Button labels are 16 to 18px heavy, just under the "large bold text" threshold of 18.66px, so strictly they fail AA.

This is how the site is built today and is recorded as is. A fix (a slightly darker pink for text and fills) is a decision for the brand owners.

Rules that follow from this

  • Keep pink text on white, never on the pale tints.
  • Use purple and orange as text only on navy.
  • Body copy is always navy or #3E4070.

Layout

Grid, spacing, shape

Grid

Desktop frame 1440px with a 1200px content column, so 120px side gutters. Mobile frame 375px with 16px gutters.

120px between sections on the Home page, every time.

Spacing inside components

Steps were set per component, not on a general grid.

8 tag padding · 10 small button · 13 input · 15 tag gap · 16 inset and mobile gutter · 20 small button sides · 40 big button sides

Shape

  • Square corners on every button, tag, input and card. No rounded radius exists.
  • One stroke weight: 2px.
  • One shadow: 4px 4px 30px at 8% black, for blog and job cards.

Signature

The offset outline

Every pink button and every slider arrow has a second 2px pink outline drawn 4px right and 4px down, behind it. It reads like a print misregistration, and after the logo it is the most recognisable detail on the site.

  • The outline is never filled, never another colour, never offset in another direction.
  • Primary: pink fill, white uppercase label. One per view.
  • Secondary: white fill, pink label and border. The second action beside a primary.
  • Big: 18px label, 40px sides, 50px tall. Small: 16px label, 20px sides, 40px tall.
  • Hover and pressed turn button and outline darker pink (#CC085D, white on it passes AA); pressed drops the outline. Disabled is all grey.

Pattern and imagery

Texture, people, product

Line waves

Large organic shapes made of dozens of thin concentric lines in a very light navy or purple tint, bleeding off the edges of hero and contact sections. Texture only: always behind content, never behind body text at full strength.

Shown above at their on-site strength, which is deliberately faint. Files: design-system/assets/patterns/ (transparent PNG). Vector originals stay in Figma.

Colour orbs and gradient

Soft purple or orange orbs sit half off a section edge, at most two per section. The one gradient (orange to purple) is only for the bar on pull quotes and the areas-of-expertise graphic.

Brand gradient, as the pull-quote bar

Product and icons

Real team photos, never stock, in tilted frames. Case studies show the client's product in a laptop or phone frame on a pale tint panel.

Icons are thin pink line drawings (person, people, shield, diamond for the values) and small single-colour technology marks.

Creatives · Proposed

Social, ads, covers, email

Twelve image templates apply the identity to social posts, stories, link images, article covers, event promos, quote cards, the LinkedIn banner, email headers and three display ad sizes. A render script fills them with copy and exports PNGs, and warns when copy is too long.

Proposed: the Figma file has no creatives, so these rules extend the website identity and need brand-owner sign-off. Files: creatives/.

  • One idea: eyebrow, headline, one sentence, one action.
  • Logo top-left, supplied file only; about 6.5% safe margin.
  • Navy display headline, short; pink only on the button.
  • One wave, at most two orbs, behind content and off an edge.
  • Real photos in the tilted frame; copy and numbers from the content guide.

Before reuse

Things to fix, found in the current file

  1. Pink fails text contrast at 4.17:1 (page 5).
  2. MUFG logo in the Home page client strip. Blocked from any public use until written consent exists.
  3. Mockup copy breaks the content guide: "top-level", "top notch", "true code masters", "awesome devs", "empower".
  4. Unsourced numbers in the stats strips ("10+", "40+", "50+", "80%", "100+ partners", "15 countries") and the Clutch rating are not in the claims register.
  5. Placeholder duplication: seven identical job cards, repeated contact-card text.
  6. Real client quotes from Clutch in testimonial cards need consent and attribution checks.
  7. Copyright line reads 2022.

Gaps in the source file

  • Iosevka Extended font file (standard width bundled).
  • No standalone mark, favicon, navy logo or dark theme.
  • No focus state (an accessible focus ring was added).
  • Large wave artwork supplied as PNG; vectors stay in Figma.
  • No photography bundled.
  • Creative rules are proposed, pending sign-off.
